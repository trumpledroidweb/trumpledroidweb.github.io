About Dr. Marcus

"Heart disease is 80% preventable when targeted with precision analytics."

With over eighteen years dedicated to clinical cardiology and preventative longevity, my focus is bridging proactive diagnostics with deeply compassionate patient-centered relationships.

Rather than waiting for acute events like chest pain or stroke, we employ advanced plaque imaging (CT Calcium scoring), genetic lipid markers (ApoB, Lp(a)), continuous hemodynamic tracking, and metabolic optimization. Every patient leaves our clinic with an actionable, personalized roadmap.
